Request Account Deletion

What to include

To help us identify your account and process your request, please send the request from the email address associated with your ManFound account and include:

  1. Your ManFound account email address.
  2. A clear statement that you want your account deleted.
  3. Any additional information reasonably necessary to verify account ownership.

What happens after your request

ManFound will review the request and take appropriate steps to delete the account and associated personal information, subject to applicable legal, security, fraud-prevention, and safety-related retention requirements.

Some information may be retained where necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce our Terms, prevent abuse, or preserve legitimate safety and security records.
