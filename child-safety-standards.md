# ManFound Child Safety Standards

Version 1.0 — Effective July 29, 2026

## Adults only

ManFound is restricted to users aged 18 or older. Profile setup requires date
of birth, the app validates age, and PostgreSQL rejects future dates and dates
belonging to a person under 18. Do not create an account for a minor, misstate
age, or invite a minor through another person's account.

## Zero tolerance

ManFound prohibits:

- Child sexual abuse or exploitation in any form.
- Grooming a child for sexual exploitation, contact, trafficking, or exchange
  of sexual material.
- Sexualization of minors, sextortion, sexual solicitation involving minors,
  or content facilitating abuse.
- Creating, uploading, requesting, possessing, linking to, storing, or
  distributing child sexual abuse material, including realistic generated
  depictions where prohibited.
- Using profiles, communities, messages, meetups, or travel to locate, recruit,
  endanger, or exploit a child.

## Reporting, blocking, and enforcement

Users can report meetup content, community posts, and direct or group messages
through available safety actions. Blocking prevents new direct messages and
connection requests while prior history may be preserved as evidence.

ManFound may restrict accounts, preserve evidence, and report suspected child
sexual abuse material or exploitation where legally required. Staff access to
private messages is limited to specifically reported content and nearby context
and is audit logged. Do not download or redistribute suspected material to
report it.
