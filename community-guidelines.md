# ManFound Community Guidelines

Version 1.0 — Effective July 29, 2026

ManFound is for adult men to form respectful friendships, learn, travel, and
meet in real life. Be honest, respect boundaries, and remember nobody owes
another person a reply, connection, invitation, or explanation.

## Prohibited conduct

- Harassment, stalking, threats, coercion, humiliation, or sharing private
  information without permission.
- Hate or discrimination based on protected characteristics.
- Sexual violence, non-consensual sexual content, exploitation, trafficking,
  grooming, sexualization of minors, child sexual abuse, or child sexual abuse
  material.
- Credible threats, glorification of violence, dangerous-event organization,
  scams, impersonation, spam, deceptive fundraising, pyramid schemes, financial
  manipulation, illegal goods, malware, or security bypass attempts.

## Meetups, travel, and finance

Describe events truthfully, disclose costs and material changes, prefer safe
public venues, and never pressure attendance, drinking, spending, room sharing,
or precise-location disclosure. Organizers must respond responsibly to safety
concerns. Financial discussion must not promise returns or solicit passwords,
codes, wallet keys, account access, or pressured transfers.

## Reporting, blocking, and enforcement

Safety actions exist on meetup details, community posts, and direct or group
messages. Reports enter the moderation queue. Blocking prevents direct
connection requests and new direct messages but may not hide all shared
community or event content. Staff can review reports and limited reported
message context and may warn, restrict, suspend, ban, or moderate content.

ManFound is not an emergency service. Contact local emergency services for
immediate danger, preserve evidence when safe, and use in-app reporting.
