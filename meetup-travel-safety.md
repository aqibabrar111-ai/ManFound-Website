# In-Person Meetup and Travel Safety Guidelines

Version 1.0 — Effective July 29, 2026

## Before meeting

- Review the organizer, description, venue, timing, cost, and available attendee
  information.
- Prefer public, staffed, well-lit first-meeting locations; tell a trusted
  person where you are going and when you expect to return.
- Arrange independent transport and an exit plan. Control your identification,
  phone, money, medication, and travel documents.
- Avoid sharing home, hotel-room, or live location details unless you understand
  and accept the risk.

## During a meetup

Respect consent, personal space, accessibility, and anyone's choice to leave.
Watch drinks and belongings; do not pressure drinking, spending, or moving to a
different venue. Check in only when present, and organizers must not falsely
confirm attendance. Leave and contact local emergency services for imminent
danger.

## Travel and online events

Independently verify entry rules, visas, insurance, health requirements,
transport, lodging, and local law. Verify bookings directly and agree on costs,
cancellations, room arrangements, and emergency contacts. ManFound is not a
booking, insurance, or emergency-assistance service.

Open online meeting links carefully. Never share passwords, one-time codes,
financial credentials, or private documents. ManFound accepts valid HTTPS
meeting links but does not operate linked meeting providers.

Use available in-app actions to report meetup or message content and block
direct contact. Preserve screenshots, receipts, and event details when safe.
