# ManFound Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 — Effective July 29, 2026

## Who this policy covers

This policy explains how ManFound, operated by `[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]` at
`[BUSINESS ADDRESS]`, handles information in the ManFound mobile app. ManFound
is a global social app for adult men to form real-world connections through
meetups, financial-literacy discussions, communities, travel discovery, and
messaging. ManFound is restricted to people aged 18 or older.

## Information ManFound handles

- Account data: email, Supabase authentication identifiers, and Google account
  information when Google sign-in is selected.
- Profile data: display name, date of birth, calculated age, biography, avatar,
  city, country, region, time zone, distance preference, and privacy settings.
- Location data: entered locations; device coordinates after a separate
  permission and explicit refresh; and event venue details.
- Social activity: communities, posts, likes, meetups, travel content, reviews,
  RSVPs, check-ins, attendance, icebreakers, and connections.
- Communications: direct and meetup-group messages and memberships.
- Safety data: blocks, reports, reported content, limited reported-message
  context, moderation actions, and staff audit logs.
- Notifications and media: preferences, push token/device metadata, avatars,
  and meetup cover images when those features are used.

## Uses

ManFound uses this information to create and secure accounts; provide profiles,
communities, meetups, travel, check-in, connections, messaging, place search,
and notifications; enforce the 18+ rule; investigate reports; prevent abuse;
and operate and improve the app.

## Visibility

Public profiles are designed to expose calculated age, display name, avatar,
bio, city, and country—not full birth date or exact profile coordinates.
Private-profile settings restrict profile-row visibility through Supabase Row
Level Security, but deliberately published posts, meetups, reviews, and chat
content remain visible to their intended audiences. Avatar storage currently
uses publicly readable URLs. Joined meetup members can read earlier group-chat
messages, and eligible connections can exchange direct messages.

## Providers and device permissions

Supabase supplies authentication, database, storage, and realtime services.
Expo supports builds and push-token registration. Geoapify processes place
autocomplete. Google processes Google sign-in. Operating-system providers
control native permissions and push delivery. The inspected app contains no ad
SDK or payment processor.

Location, notification, camera, and media-library permissions are separate from
legal acceptance and are requested by their related features.

## Retention, deletion, and security

Data remains while needed to operate the account, provide features, preserve
safety evidence, or meet legitimate legal and security needs. Prior message
history can be retained after blocking or disconnection. ManFound does not yet
have a complete self-service deletion flow. Contact `[SUPPORT EMAIL]` until a
verified deletion process and retention schedule are implemented.

ManFound uses Supabase authentication, RLS, owner-scoped policies, and
restricted staff functions. No system guarantees absolute security. Because
ManFound is global, information may be processed outside a user's country; the
operator must document hosting and transfer mechanisms before publication.
